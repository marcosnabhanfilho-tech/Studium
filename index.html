About

Studium Generale is the medieval name for a university — a studium open to scholars from everywhere. This one is a reel: swipe through folios drawn from Aquinas, Chesterton, Newman, Scruton, Mises, Hayek, Buffett, Munger, Vermeer, Caravaggio, Mozart, Doyle, and Burke.

A folio you view is permanently added to its Wing. Double-tap to keep one in your Thesaurus. Finish ten folios in a day to keep the Rule. There is no infinite scroll — once the Rule is kept, the day's work is done.
